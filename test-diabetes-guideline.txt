NICE Diabetes Guidelines - Blood Glucose Management

Introduction
This document outlines the key principles for managing blood glucose levels in adults with diabetes in care home settings.

Blood Glucose Targets
- Pre-meal glucose: 4-7 mmol/L
- Post-meal glucose: <9 mmol/L after 2 hours
- HbA1c target: <53 mmol/mol (7.0%) for most adults

Hypoglycemia Management
Symptoms include sweating, trembling, confusion, and altered consciousness.

Treatment:
1. If conscious and able to swallow: Give 15-20g fast-acting carbohydrate
2. Recheck glucose after 10-15 minutes
3. If still below 4 mmol/L, repeat treatment
4. Once glucose >4 mmol/L, give long-acting carbohydrate

Emergency situations requiring glucagon or IV glucose should be escalated to medical professionals immediately.

Medication Administration
- Always check blood glucose before insulin administration
- Rotate injection sites to prevent lipodystrophy
- Store insulin at 2-8°C when not in use
- Never shake insulin - roll gently between hands

Documentation Requirements
All blood glucose readings, medication administration, and hypoglycemic episodes must be documented in the resident's care plan and reported to the GP as per CQC requirements.
