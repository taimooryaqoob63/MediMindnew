Carbon dioxide (CO2) is a colorless, odorless gas that is naturally present in Earth's atmosphere. It is produced by the respiration of animals and plants, the combustion of organic matter, and various industrial processes. In the human body, carbon dioxide is a waste product of metabolism and is expelled from the body through the respiratory system. In healthcare, understanding carbon dioxide levels is crucial, particularly in respiratory and metabolic assessments. Abnormal CO2 levels can indicate respiratory issues or metabolic imbalances, which may require medical intervention. For example, elevated carbon dioxide levels in the blood, a condition known as hypercapnia, can result from respiratory disorders such as chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
