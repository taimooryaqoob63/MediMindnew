Understanding Hypoglycemia vs. Hyperglycemia In diabetes care, recognizing the difference between **hypoglycemia** and **hyperglycemia** is crucial for effective management and patient safety. ### Hypoglycemia (Low Blood Sugar) **Definition**: Hypoglycemia occurs when blood sugar levels drop below normal, typically below 4 mmol/L. ### Symptoms:
MildMild*: Sweating, trembling, hunger, anxiety
SevereSevere*: Confusion, drowsiness, unconsciousness ### Causes:
Excessive insulin or medication
Skipping meals
Increased physical activity without adjusting food intake ### Immediate Action:

Increased thirst
Frequent urination
Fatigue
Blurred vision ### Causes:
Insufficient insulin or medication
Overeating, particularly carbohydrates
Stress or illness ### Management:
