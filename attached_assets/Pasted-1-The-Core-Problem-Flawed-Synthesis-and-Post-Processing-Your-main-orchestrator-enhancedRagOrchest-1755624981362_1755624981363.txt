1. The Core Problem: Flawed Synthesis and Post-Processing
Your main orchestrator, enhancedRagOrchestrator.ts, runs multiple agents in parallel (medical_specialist, compliance_officer, etc.) and then attempts to synthesize their outputs into a single response. This is where the issues you're seeing—repetition, bizarre formatting, and excessive length—are created.

Root Cause of Repetition: The synthesizeFinalResponse method takes the raw content from multiple agents. If both the Medical Specialist and the Learning Facilitator explain what blood glucose is, you'll get that explanation twice. The synthesizer LLM then struggles to merge them and often just includes both, leading to the severe repetition seen in your screenshots (image_fbb4df.jpg, image_fbb4a4.jpg).

Root Cause of Bizarre Formatting: When the raw, repetitive agent outputs (which may contain their own markdown) are mashed together and fed to the synthesizer, the final LLM call gets confused. It tries to clean it up but ends up creating malformed markdown like **insulin**insulin** or misplaced ## headings, as seen in image_fbb445.jpg.

2. The Solution: A Robust Cleanup and Formatting Pipeline
The good news is you've already written most of the code needed to fix this. Your system has a powerful semantic deduplication function and an excellent response formatter; they just aren't being used in the most critical step.

Here are the specific changes to implement in enhancedRagOrchestrator.ts.

Change 1: Implement Aggressive Semantic Deduplication
Your code contains a semanticDeduplicateAgentResponses method that uses embeddings to understand the meaning of sentences and remove duplicates. This is far more powerful than simple text matching. However, your main synthesis function falls back to a much simpler deduplication. You need to ensure this semantic check is always used.

Modify the synthesizeFinalResponse function to explicitly use your best deduplication logic before synthesizing.

Summary of Recommendations
Fix the Synthesis Pipeline: The core problem is in enhancedRagOrchestrator.ts. Before you synthesize the final answer, you must run the agent responses through your semanticDeduplicateAgentResponses method to eliminate repetition.

Enforce a Single, Final Formatter: At the very end of the processQuery method, take the synthesized content and pass it through ImprovedResponseFormatter.formatDiabetesResponse. This will give you the clean, structured output your users need.

Refine the Synthesizer Prompt: Strengthen the instructions for the synthesizer LLM, explicitly telling it to be concise and to avoid all repetition, as shown in the code snippet above.
