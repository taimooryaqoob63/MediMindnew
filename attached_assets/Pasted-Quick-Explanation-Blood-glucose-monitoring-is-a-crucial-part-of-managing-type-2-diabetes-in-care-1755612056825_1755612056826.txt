Quick Explanation Blood **glucose** monitoring is a crucial part of managing type 2 diabetes in care home residents. It involves checking the resident's **blood sugar** levels at specific times during the day, usually before meals and at bedtime. The aim is to keep blood **glucose** levels within a certain range, typically between 4-7 mmol/L before meals and less than 8. 5 mmol/L after meals [NICE]. For example, in a care home setting, a resident with type 2 diabetes might have their blood **glucose** checked first thing in the morning before breakfast, then again before lunch, dinner, and at bedtime.
